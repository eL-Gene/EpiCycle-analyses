# Fig. 6 and 7: human cell morphospace and niches

Panels: Fig. 6A-H, Fig. 7A-M; Videos S6, S7; Tables S3, S6.

Contents: notebooks/ 01 shape morphospace, 02 niche embedding, 03 figure panels, 04 niche figure panels, 05 placode vs ridge; stats/ (Fig. 7 I-L statistics); tools/ (`fix_step4_paths.py`).

Words used in the code vs the manuscript: see `TERMINOLOGY.md` at the repository root
(`leiden` = shape cluster, `niche_leiden_r1.5` of `hop3_pure` = niche cluster, `area` = cell volume).

The input is the per-cell feature table of the human stacks segmented in the EpiCycle plugin's
stack-segmentation step (Step 4), with the zebrafish-to-human pair of `../fig5_mammalian_epithelia/`,
plus that Step 4 workspace for the contact graphs, the label volumes and the cell meshes. Notebook 01 builds the
shape morphospace, 02 the niche embedding over the cell contact graph; 03, 04 and 05 read their
pickles back and draw the panels; `stats/` holds the four-group statistics of Fig. 7 I-L (see
`stats/README.md`).

## Settings as run

Notebook 01 (shape morphospace):

- 31 shape features (`TARGET_FEATURES`); each is log1p-transformed when its minimum is >= 0 and
  its absolute skew > 1, then scaled with `RobustScaler` and clipped at +-5.
- PCA to 10 components (residual PCA off); a 15-nearest-neighbour graph (Euclidean) on the PCs;
  Leiden at resolutions 0.3, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5 and 3.0; the shape clusters of the figures
  are resolution 1.0. UMAP with 15 neighbours, minimum distance 0.1, Euclidean.
- 10 representative cells per cluster and resolution. Seed 42 for PCA, Leiden and UMAP.

Notebook 02 (niches): each cell's niche vector is its PC vector averaged over the contact graph
of its stack (edges with at least 100 contact voxels, weighted by contact), iterated 1 to 10 times
with a self weight of 0, 0.3, 0.5 or 0.7: 40 configurations, each clustered with Leiden at the
same seven resolutions (280 clusterings) and embedded with UMAP (15 neighbours, minimum distance
0.1). The figures use `hop3_pure` (three iterations, self weight 0) at resolution 1.5. Seed 42.

Notebook 05 compares the hair placode (niche cluster 17 of `(13 wks) Front skin`, against its
surrounding epidermis, cluster 0) with the ridge basal cells (niche clusters 3, 7, 10, 13 and 14
of `(13 wks) D3L ventral ridges`, against the other cells of that stack), with 2,000 bootstrap
resamples (seed 0). It checks that the four groups hold 393 (surrounding epidermis), 276 (hair placode),
1,903 (ridge basal cells) and 1,006 (other cells of the ridge stack) cells before going on.

## Notes

- Notebook 02 raises an error when no stack of the feature table has an adjacency table, and
  prints a warning with the count when some stacks have none, since those cells get no niche
  coordinates.
- The last cells of notebook 04 open napari windows to export Fig. 6H, so they need a display; on a
  machine without one, stop before the cell that starts napari.

`python -m pytest tests -q` from the repository root runs notebook 01 and 02 on a 60-cell-per-stack
sample of the feature table with a synthetic workspace, then the loading cells of 03, 04 and 05 on
what they wrote, and checks the Fig. 7 group sizes (`tests/test_fig6_7_chain.py`).

## Opening the workspace in the EpiCycle plugin

To open the workspace in the EpiCycle plugin, copy it and run
`python fig6_7_human_morphospace/tools/fix_step4_paths.py <copy>` once. It writes absolute paths for
your machine into the copy. Never run it on the folder the notebooks read. To export the merged table
from the plugin, press Finalize first.
